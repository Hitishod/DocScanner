Privacy policy

Last updated:

The short version

Docverse does not have accounts, does not show ads, and does not send your documents to us or to any server run by us. Your scans stay on your phone.

What the app stores

  • Scans, pages and files you create are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Settings (theme, default quality, text language) are stored on your device.
  • When you tap "Save to Downloads", a copy is placed in your Downloads folder. You choose when this happens.

Permissions and Google components

  • Camera and scanning: scanning uses Google's ML Kit document scanner, provided through Google Play services. Docverse does not receive your camera images except the pages you choose to keep.
  • Text recognition and QR scanning: these also use Google ML Kit on your device. Google's components can download updates and may collect technical information as described in Google's ML Kit data disclosure.
  • Photos and files: the app only opens pictures and PDFs that you pick yourself.

Sharing

Documents leave your phone only when you use Share or Save. Docverse does not share your data with third parties.

Web version

The browser version at /app works entirely inside your browser tab. Files you open are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to any server. Docverse Web has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. Closing the tab clears everything.

Children

Docverse does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
